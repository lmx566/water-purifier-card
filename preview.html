Shared scenario controls update all three cards. The editor and selected-card options apply only to the card you choose. All readings and history are simulated; no purifier, MQTT broker or Home Assistant server is contacted.

Preview controls stay in English. Cards and the editor follow the simulated HA language unless their own language option overrides it. Filter abbreviations are custom names and remain unchanged.
